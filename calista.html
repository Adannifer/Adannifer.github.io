Calista by Cheyenne Tsai New York Bridal Market Freelance Photographer

Overview

Reels and social photos for a New York bridal couture label, captured on the floor at New York Bridal Market.

As freelance photographer for Calista by Cheyenne Tsai, I shot the booth across fittings, buyer appointments and quiet moments between shows. The footage became three vertical Reels and a set of branded photos for Instagram, each signed with the label's script logo.
