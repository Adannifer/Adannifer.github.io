Research

Gen Z stopped chewing gum. They didn't stop collecting charms.

Gum is a forgettable purchase: every pack looks the same and ends up in the trash. Meanwhile, Gen Z keeps buying and stacking bag charms. Our question was simple: what if gum became a charm?

Process

Every big decision, from name to hinge.

01Two aesthetic directions

A soft, pearly, healing look (pearls, pastels, satin) and a bold Y2K look (high contrast, rhinestones, flash photography). The pearly look became the product base; the Y2K energy lives in the campaign and social content.

02Naming and logo

Our first name, Wishpop, was already used by a similar product, so we dropped it to avoid infringement risk. Chewel was taken and Gummypop sat too close to existing candy brands, so we landed on Poppumi. The wordmark samples chubby, cloud-like puffy lettering, with a small star above the i.

03A real case, not just a cute render

We set the puffy star shape first, then added a clear, bubble-like shell so it reads as gum. To make it usable, I sketched how the case opens by hand and fed the sketch to the AI agent. The final case has two openings: a small flip cap on the top point to shake out gum, and a full clamshell for refills, with the hinge on the left point and a press latch on the right.

Directing AI

We acted as creative directors and let Lovart's agent be the design team.

How we directed it

  • Detailed prompts with hex codes, materials and exact placement.
  • References uploaded for material only, never other brands' images.
  • One variable at a time: shape, then material, then color, then scene.
  • Hand sketches for product mechanics; closed shots edited into the open state so materials matched.
  • A human layer in Figma for the logo, card text and final layouts.

What fought back

  • The AI kept redrawing or misspelling the logo, so we placed the original file by hand.
  • The pearlescent case picked up every scene's color.
  • The star shape drifted between generations.
  • Small text on cards and labels came out garbled.

What we cut

Satin charm, v1

Too decorative, extra ribbons and a weak star. Stripped back to shape and material.

Bedazzled jelly case

Clearly read as gum, but looked like a toy. We kept the idea of visible function.
