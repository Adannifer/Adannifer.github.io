US Market Rebrand · Shopify Site · Visual System Remote — 2025–2026 Visual Designer

Overview

Freelance visual designer for Joydotz, a hydrocolloid spot-patch brand, through Swedish Falls LLC. The brand was repositioning for the US market with a Gen Z audience in mind, and the existing site still read as a generic acne-treatment product. I rebuilt the color system, the product and site visuals, and the site's interaction design around a softer, more collectible identity that fit how that audience actually shops.

Blind Box Characters

Unbuilt Concept

Developed with the Joydotz team: sell the patches as blind boxes, giving each of the three patch shapes (flower, cloud, butterfly) an original character in the style of Sailor Moon or My Little Pony.

Future product drops would become "skins" of those three starter characters rather than entirely new SKUs, modeled on Pop Mart's approach, to deepen brand storytelling for the Gen Z audience beyond "just a daily patch."

Reflection

Impact

Took Joydotz from a default Shopify skin into a cohesive, interactive brand system built for its US Gen Z audience: a packaging-derived color palette, a shape-based product language, and a mascot moment that carries the identity to the footer.

Reflection

Working from an established brand rather than a blank page meant finding the fresh angle already hiding in the packaging, the color gradient, the shape language, instead of inventing one from scratch, then carrying it consistently across every touchpoint.
