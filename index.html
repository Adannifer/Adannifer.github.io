About

Every flip lands on something real.

I'm a New York-based designer who moves between brand identity, motion, and physical space. I studied Interactive Media Arts at NYU Tisch, which is where I learned to treat design as something people can walk into, not just look at.

Outside client work, I run AllKnight NYC and CrimePartner, so I spend as much time building the culture I design for as I do designing it. I'm drawn to projects where a poster, a party, and a piece of code all have to work together.
