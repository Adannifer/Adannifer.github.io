About

JenniferQian★

Brand & Visual Designer

Every flip lands on something real.

Jennifer Qian is a New York-based designer who moves between brand identity, motion and physical space. She studied Interactive Media Arts at NYU Tisch, where she learned to treat design as something people can walk into, not just look at.

Outside client work, she runs AllKnight NYC and CrimePartner, so she spends as much time building the culture she designs for as designing it. She is drawn to projects where a poster, a party and a piece of code all have to work together.
