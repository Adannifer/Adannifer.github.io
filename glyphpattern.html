GLYPH★PATTERN

One glyph Positive & negative space NYU · 2025

Overview

One letter J, tiled until the gaps between letters draw a second shape of their own.

The brief: use a single glyph to build a pattern where positive and negative space read equally well, then make two images that are exact opposites of each other. I tested a script J from Deseo Medium against a blackletter J from Ziyoudian, a Chinese typeface, then mirrored and rotated the Deseo curve until the pattern locked.
