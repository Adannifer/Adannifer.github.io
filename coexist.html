Overview

Colonial history, pirate archetypes and occupation-era fashion, collaged into one Hong Kong.

A collage series created during a summer school at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, exploring how colonial history shaped Hong Kong's visual and cultural identity. The work uses color collision and figure fragmentation to visualize coexistence: of cultures, of eras, of power.
