HEADSPACE★ONBOARDING

Cutting a 6-minute onboarding to 3, and making the breathing moment carry the message instead of the ads.

Group project · Haley, Jindi, JenniferFigma · 1 weekMy part: breathing animation + microcopy

Overview

Headspace is a meditation app people open when they are stressed. Its onboarding makes them sit through ads first.

What Headspace is

A guided meditation and sleep app with a calm, cartoon-led brand and a signature "breathe in, breathe out" opening.

The problem

Onboarding runs about 6 minutes and 17 screens: three App Store style promo pages, a sign-up wall, a goal picker that changes nothing, then a long breathing exercise.

Why it hurts

The people opening the app are anxious or in a hurry. Ads in the first seconds read as "we want your money, not your calm," and the drop-off happens before the first real session.

Group work with Haley and Jindi on research and discussion. I owned the breathing-animation copy and the microcopy pass. Earlier onboarding screens via App Fuel.

Define

Opportunities

One question, three places to act.

How might we

let an anxious person reach their first calm moment in half the time, without losing what Headspace needs to say?

01Remove

the three App Store style promo screens and anything priced before sign-up.

02Merge

the promo message into the breathe-in / breathe-out animation users already like.

03Connect

goal selection to a visibly different next screen.

Goal6 min → ~3 minand keep attention unbroken to the home screen
Develop

Ideation

Early directions on the flow itself.

Shorter

No ads before sign-up; delete three promo screens.

Honest

A personalization flow that visibly changes recommendations.

Memorable

Make breathing in and out the one thing people remember.

Early sketchesRough paper ideas coming here.

Keeping the Headspace voice

We matched the heading and body type so the new copy sits inside the app without a seam, and tested which promo line was worth keeping: "Stress less, sleep soundly" over "Everything your mind needs."

Deliver

Final design

Three changes, before and after.

01Three promo pages, gone

They looked like App Store previews, broke the cartoon style, had nothing to tap, and repeated later screens.

02The message rides the breath

Key lines from the deleted pages move into the opening breathe-in / breathe-out pop-up. The animation runs a little longer; context stays, flow stays smooth. My part.

03Goals that actually do something

Pick "Reduce stress" and "Manage anxiety", and the next screen highlights a grounding exercise for anxiety, labelled so it reads as recommended, not generic.

Deliver

Testing

12 people walked the new flow: classmates, ITP students and Design Lab.

Held up
  • Everyone agreed the three promo pages should go
  • "I love the combo of the breathing exercise."
  • "Love the idea of adding the animation for the leading page."
  • Shorter, less time-consuming
Pushback
  • One tester felt text inside the breath was "being controlled by the company"
  • People wanted to see course titles under the goal they picked
  • Hierarchy and UX writing on the activity list still too even
Not yet validated

The 3-minute target is estimated from screen count, not timed with users. Next round: time the same 12 people end to end, and test the breath-text with and without copy.

Learned

Audit fast

A full UX audit is possible in a week if you walk the flow as a user, not a designer.

Flow over screens

Clarity and continuity beat extra marketing screens, especially in a wellness app.

Microcopy is personalization

"For you" and a matching title did more than any new feature.
